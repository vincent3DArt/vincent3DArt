VINH'S SNORLAX INTRO

Custom 13.2-second animation, typography, effects and timing created for Vinh.
The two phrases are exactly:
Hi my name is Vinh!
But people spell it "B-E-N"...

Character design and sprite artwork: CHUNSOFT, via PMDCollab SpriteCollab.
Source: https://github.com/PMDCollab/SpriteCollab/tree/967e03358f6c41ff58546af74d7dc21e39a8fa93/sprite/0143
The chunky Mystery Dungeon design matches the user's selected reference.
Original Idle, Hop and Stomp frames provide the standing, wake-up and slam poses.
Front, side and back Rotate frames texture a reconstructed pixel-rendered volume
for the prone roll and sit-up transition. The head-to-feet body axis points away
from the viewer. Turning this volume shows belly, side, back and opposite side;
the animation does not spin a single flat sprite in the picture plane.
All character art comes from this one source set. No Emerald sprites are used.
