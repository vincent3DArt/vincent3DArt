VINH'S SNORLAX INTRO

Custom 13.2-second animation, typography, effects and timing created for Vinh.
The two phrases are exactly:
Hi my name is Vinh!
But people spell it "B-E-N"...

Snorlax pixel sprites: CHUNSOFT, via the PMDCollab SpriteCollab archive.
Source: https://github.com/PMDCollab/SpriteCollab/tree/967e03358f6c41ff58546af74d7dc21e39a8fa93/sprite/0143
Frames used: Idle, Hop, Stomp. Character frames are extracted,
scaled with nearest-neighbor sampling, rotated and sequenced for this banner.
Background, lettering, dust trail, particles, cracks and animation timing are custom.

Feet-facing sleeping/rolling pose: Pokemon Emerald / GAME FREAK, via PokeAPI.
Source: https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites/blob/master/sprites/pokemon/versions/generation-iii/emerald/143.png
The reclining source pose is palette-matched to the other sprites, resized,
and animated from the foot end, keeping both soles directed toward the viewer.
